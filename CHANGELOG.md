# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-08-25

### What ships

- A bilingual Chinese/English experience built around 32 everyday scenarios, six stable core dimensions, and two recent-state signals.
- Transparent, language-neutral scoring metadata with fixed archetype centers, adjacent-facet context, and tested result assembly.
- Browser-local progress, language preference, and up to three recent results, with migration and complete FACET-data deletion.
- A bilingual Canvas share card generated and downloaded locally, plus a self-contained HTML build that also runs from `file://`.
- Keyboard navigation, visible focus, 44px controls, narrow-screen coverage, and reduced-motion behavior.

### Privacy

- No account, analytics, telemetry, tracking pixel, remote font, or answer upload.
- Quiz progress and result history stay in localStorage until cleared; share images stay on the device unless deliberately shared.

### Boundaries

- FACET is an entertainment and self-reflection experience, not a peer-reviewed, clinical, or diagnostic instrument.
- Results are not suitable for hiring, career decisions, relationship verdicts, or claims about rarity and accuracy.
- Chinese and English share IDs, scoring directions, thresholds, and archetype centers; this does not establish cross-cultural psychometric equivalence.
- Chromium is the automated browser target for this release. Other current browsers are expected to work but are not part of the automated matrix.
